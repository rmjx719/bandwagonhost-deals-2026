## How BandwagonHost discounts actually work

Most hosting brands advertise a steep first-term discount and then quietly raise the renewal price. BandwagonHost does the opposite, and understanding this changes how you should shop.

Their promo codes are small (usually 5–7%) but **recurring**: once a code is applied to a service, the same percentage keeps applying on every renewal invoice for the life of that VPS. You don't re-enter anything. A 6.77% code on the $49.99/year entry plan brings it to $46.61 at checkout, and it stays at that level on renewal. Over three years that's real money, just not headline money.

The flip side: BandwagonHost rarely runs the "80% off" style flash sales you see elsewhere, because the base prices are already lean. There's no inflated list price to discount from. The entry KVM plan at $49.99/year and the CN2 GIA-E entry at $169.99/year are priced close to what the route quality actually costs, which is exactly why the recurring codes stay small.

There's a second discount mechanism most New Year sale hunters miss: **limited-edition plan restocks**. BandwagonHost periodically drops small-batch plans at deeply discounted annual prices, often a third to half of the equivalent regular plan. Recent examples that have circulated include the Fremont MINICHICKEN at $19/year, the LA BiggerBox Pro at $39/year, and the DC6 CN2 GIA-E Limited Edition at $49.99/year. Stock is irregular and sells out fast, and limited editions generally cannot be combined with coupon codes. If you can snag one during a restock, the plan price itself is the discount.
